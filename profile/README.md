## Brigham Young University - Idaho Data Wrangling and Visualization Class

This course is part of the BYU-I [Data Science Program](https://byuidatascience.github.io/). It supports multiple majors, minors, and certificates. This GitHub org is a staging space for the AI-focused transition, as the course has been taught since 2017. All our DS faculty teach AI-aided courses. For Fall 2026, this is the first AI-native attempt to teach a data science curriculum. [J. Hathaway](https://www.byui.edu/directories/j-hathaway) is taking a swing at this first. 

### Course Outcomes

Upon completion of this course, you will be able to select, produce, and defend visualizations of messy and complex data, working in partnership with AI tools while remaining accountable for what those tools produce. Specifically, as a successful learner, you will be able to:

- Recognize and name, on sight, the chart forms a given question or data structure supports as well as the inappropriate charts that confuse or mislead.
- Implement the grammar of graphics to project data onto a chosen chart form.
- Diagnose and resolve the ambiguities in messy data and in underspecified requests.
- Read and apply the vocabulary of data wrangling to translate business-driven questions into specific, executable requests.
- Evaluate AI-generated code, transformations, and visualizations for correctness, and verify results against the source data.
- Defend visualization and summarization choices against standards of honest representation and persuasion, including the responsible use of AI tools.
- Deliver data-informed projects for stakeholder questions, working in partnership with AI tools and human collaborators.
